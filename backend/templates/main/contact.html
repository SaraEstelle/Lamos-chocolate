{% trans "Why we built Lamos Chocolate" %}

{% blocktrans %}When the "Dubai chocolate" went viral, we kept coming back to the same thought: here was a maison born from real craft — Swiss Carma chocolate, Belgian Callebaut cream, Iranian Rafsanjan pistachios — and yet nothing online did it justice. A house that moves people deserved a storefront that moves people too.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}So we set ourselves a real challenge, not a school exercise: a chocolatier does not have one client, but three — the individual who wants a bar tonight, the hotel that needs four hundred engraved boxes for Christmas, and the owner who has to decide what to produce and when. We wanted to serve all three from a single platform, with one stock and one source of truth.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}We built it in about a month, as two developers, learning the hard and rewarding parts of e-commerce, payments and Swiss data protection along the way. It is the project we are proudest of — and the one that taught us the most.{% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %}Lamos Chocolate is a Portfolio Project developed at{% endblocktrans %} Holberton School.
